Biography

👋 Hi, I'm Boshi Zhang, a Ph.D. student in the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University 🎓, where I also earned my B.Eng. in Control Science and Engineering.

🌍 My research centers on world models, which let machines imagine before they act, together with efficient generative modeling ⚡. Lately I'm most excited about agentic robotics 🦾, robots that plan, act and keep improving in the real world.
