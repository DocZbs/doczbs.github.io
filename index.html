✦ ABOUT

Understanding
from first principles.

I am Boshi Zhang, a graduate of the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University. My work connects world models, efficient generative modeling, and reinforcement learning for agents.

I am interested in how intelligent systems can imagine what comes next, learn from experience, and continuously improve. Recently, I have worked on modular embodied skills, engineering agents, and safe flow matching.
